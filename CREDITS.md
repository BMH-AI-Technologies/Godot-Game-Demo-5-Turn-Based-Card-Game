# Demo assets

The project keeps the assets used by the demo, their editable art sources and supplied licenses. Unused pack contents have been removed.

| Used asset | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Knight | Supplied 2D_SL_Knight_v1.0 pack. [License](assets/2D_SL_Knight_v1.0/license.txt). Idle, Attacks, Hurt, Death and Pray sheets use 128×64 frames; SpriteFrames are in assets/animations/knight_frames.tres. |
| Green Slime | Brackeys platformer assets, analogStudios_; CC0. 24×24 frames, with idle, attack, hurt and death cues. |
| Skeleton Soldier | Supplied `assets/Skeleton enemy/Skeleton enemy.png` and its Aseprite source. 64×64 cells; idle, single-swing attack, double-swing attack, hurt, death and cast in `assets/animations/skeleton_frames.tres`. |
| Iron Brute | Heavy Bandit from the supplied Bandits pack by Sven Thole. [EULA](assets/Bandits/EULA.txt). 48×48 frames, with combat idle, attack, hurt, death and recovery/cast cues. |
| Veiled Assassin | Supplied NightBorne sprite sheet, assets/NightBorne/NightBorne.png. 80×80 frames; editable idle, attack, hurt, death and cast animations in assets/animations/assassin_frames.tres. |
| Card artwork | Shikashi's Fantasy Icons Pack v2, supplied #2 - Transparent Icons & Drop Shadow.png; 32×32 AtlasTextures. Pack notes permit commercial use/remixing and credit designs from game-icons.net under CC BY 3.0. |
| Music | Brackeys / Sofia Thirslund, time_for_adventure.mp3; CC0. |
| Be Vietnam Pro | [Font license](assets/fonts/OFL.txt), SIL Open Font License. |
| Combat sounds | Supplied `assets/Sounds/Minifantasy_Dungeon_SFX` (player sword swing), `10_Battle_SFX` (impact/block), `8_Buffs_Heals_SFX` (heal/buff/debuff), and `10_UI_Menu_SFX` (mana/interrupt) packs. |
| Forest, UI/result sound cues | Original demo scenery and generated audio cues. |

Brackeys terms and credits: [LICENSE & CREDITS.txt](<assets/brackeys_platformer_assets/LICENSE & CREDITS.txt>).

Actor feet pivots, scales and AnimatedSprite2D children are editable in their scenes. Knight frames include eight idle, ten attack, three nonblank hurt, four death and twelve casting frames. Skeleton's basic clip excludes the second swing; Bone Cleave selects the full two-swing clip through its skill resource. Frame durations place the swings near the separately presented damage hits.

Shikashi pack notes and original credits: [Shikashi's Fantasy Icons Pack.txt](<assets/Shikashi's Fantasy Icons Pack v2/Shikashi's Fantasy Icons Pack v2/Shikashi's Fantasy Icons Pack.txt>).
